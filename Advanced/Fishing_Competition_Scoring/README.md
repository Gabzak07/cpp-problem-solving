# Fishing_Competition_Scoring

Calculates scores for a fishing tournament based on fish species and weight, while also aggregating the total catch weight for each eligible species.

Input: `Duomenys.txt` — participant details including their names and caught fish with weights, followed by a scoring table for specific fish species.  
Output: `Rezultatas.txt` — a sorted leaderboard of participants by total points, and a sorted summary of total weight caught per fish species.
