# Camp_Participant_Selection

Calculates the minimum and maximum performance scores of camp participants and filters the roster to find those who meet a specific qualifying threshold.

Input: `Duomenys.txt` — total number of participants, the qualifying score threshold, and a list of participant IDs with their respective scores.  
Output: `Rezultatas.txt` — the lowest and highest scores overall, followed by a list of IDs for the participants who met or exceeded the threshold.
