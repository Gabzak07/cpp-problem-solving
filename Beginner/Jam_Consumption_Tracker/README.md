# Jam_Consumption_Tracker

Simulates the consumption of a jar of jam by different characters, each eating a specific amount per visit, to determine who finishes it or how much remains.

Input: `Duomenys2.txt` — initial amount of jam, total number of visits, and a character sequence representing who visited the kitchen.  
Output: `Rezultatas.txt` — the remaining amount of jam and either the name of the character who took the last portion, or a "liko" (leftover) status.
