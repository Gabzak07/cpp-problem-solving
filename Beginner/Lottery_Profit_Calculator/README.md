# Lottery_Profit_Calculator

Calculates the total expenses, total winnings, and net profit from a series of lottery ticket purchases. It also determines the maximum single win and the total number of tickets bought.

Input: `Duomenys.txt` — number of purchase records, followed by the amount spent and the amount won for each record.  
Output: `Rezultatas.txt` — total spent and won, the maximum single win, total tickets purchased, and the overall net profit.
