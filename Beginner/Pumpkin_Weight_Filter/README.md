# Pumpkin_Weight_Filter

Calculates the overall average weight of a pumpkin harvest and filters out the pumpkins that meet specific weight requirements for commercial purchase.

Input: `Duomenys.txt` — total number of pumpkins, the minimum and maximum acceptable weight limits, and a list of individual pumpkin weights.  
Output: `Rezultatas.txt` — overall average weight, the count and average weight of the accepted pumpkins, and the final list of their individual weights.
