# Apple_Pie_Recipe_Analyzer

Analyzes a list of apple pie recipes to calculate total ingredients needed and average quantities. It also counts recipes meeting specific time and temperature thresholds and filters out those requiring baking temperatures of 200 degrees or higher.

Input: `Duomenys.txt` — list of recipes with ingredient quantities and baking requirements (plus console input for time and temperature limits).  
Output: `Rezultatas.txt` — calculated totals, averages, threshold counts, and the filtered recipe list.
