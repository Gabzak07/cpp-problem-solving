# Teacher_Student_Statistics

Analyzes school data to determine the number of students per teacher, the teacher with the largest class, and the teacher with the highest student average grade.

Input: 
`Mokytojai.txt` — list of teachers and their respective subjects.
`Mokiniai.txt` — list of students, the subjects they take, and their grades.

Output: 
`Rezultatas.txt` — student count for each teacher, followed by the most popular teacher and the teacher with the highest academic average.
